Everybody's Bad Guy

Animated series · Developed with JASH for Hulu

Written by Justin Hantz
Created by Justin Hantz & Josh Freydkis

A sitcom set in the world of a 1980s fantasy-action cartoon, told from the villain's side. Mordon is an evil warlock who also happens to be an incompetent narcissist. The forces of good stand in the way of his conquests, but not as much as his own ego.

Mordon inherits the job of conquering Enduria after the hero, Valdor, kills his big brother. He summons his own band of villains. They are equally ineffectual and, frankly, dumb. Every time they get close, Valdor bursts through a wall and beats them up.

The Fires

Drama series · Pittsburgh

Written by Lee Landey & Justin Hantz

A supernatural detective drama. In present-day Pittsburgh, an unlikely pair of detectives investigate a string of arsons plaguing the rust belt. What begins as a classic procedural in the tradition of Columbo takes new form as the cosmic horror of H.P. Lovecraft creeps in.
